Dentures are permanent replacements for missing teeth and surrounding tissues can be able to taken out and put back into your mouth. If you have missing or damaged teeth, getting dentures can help you look natural and feel comfortable than ever. Routine cleaning your dentures will help to minimize odor and stains, keep your dentures looking like new and help them last longer.

We have Explained Some Tips That Will Help You Get The Dentures Healthy.

Clean Dentures Daily: Good oral health is maintained by keeping your dentures clean. Remove dentures before cleaning. Brush your dentures every day with a soft brush morning and night. Clean your dentures and leave them in cold water overnight. Also brush your gums, tongue, and natural teeth every morning and night with a soft toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.

New Dentures: Dentures can make you feel different for the first time. It may take several weeks to feel comfortable. Don’t let dentures dry out. Place them in a denture cleanser soaking solution or in plain water when you’re not wearing them. Never use hot water which can cause dentures to warp.

Remove Your Dentures Every Night: This allows the gum tissue to get a chance to rest.

Dentures should be well fitted and comfortable when you chew and speak. See our oral health professional dentists in Riverside, CA, if you have any pain or problems with your mouth or dentures or if you need further information on denture cleaning and care.
